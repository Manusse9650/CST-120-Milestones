Enter Avelis

In a world where magic lives within every beating heart, kingdoms rise beneath the watchful light of the Eternal Flame. Across Avelis, stories of love, devotion, betrayal, and sacrifice unfold, shaping the lives of rulers and ordinary people alike. From the sunlit palaces of Solarath to quiet villages nestled among the mountains, every life leaves its mark upon the world. Yet even the smallest choices can alter the course of history, and not every story remembered is the one that truly happened.

About the World

Welcome to Avelis, a world where magic is woven into the natural rhythms of life, from the roots beneath the soil to the light of the sun above. Its kingdoms are shaped by ancient traditions, sacred bonds, and the belief that every living thing carries a fragment of the Eternal Flame. Explore its landscapes, cultures, histories, and the countless lives that make this world more than the stories told within it.
