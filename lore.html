Lore Archive

The Lore Archive provides definitions and information about beliefs, traditions, locations, timekeeping, and other details found throughout Avelis.

Avelis Lore Archive
Category System Details
Religion Eternal Flame The first light and source of all life. The Eternal Flame is believed to know all, give all, and sustain all. Every Heart Flame is born from it and ultimately returns to it.
Religion Heart Flame A living fragment of the Eternal Flame entrusted to every mortal. Caring for the Heart Flame through choices and deeds is considered one of life's greatest responsibilities.
Traditions Flame Binding The sacred union of two mortals before the Eternal Flame. Through binding vows and the Handfasting, their lives are joined and their magic is bound to answer as one.
Traditions Binding Basket A woven basket prepared for a newly bound couple and filled with gifts of nourishment, comfort, and hope for the life they begin together.
Traditions Handfasting A sacred rite performed during a Flame Binding in which the hands of two mortals are bound together with a woven cord to signify their union before the Eternal Flame.
Timekeeping Solturn One complete journey of Avelis around the Eternal Flame. A Solturn consists of ten moons, forty spans, and four hundred cycles.
Locations Veshalor
The holy city of Solarath, located within a volcanic basin in the Veshalor Mountains. It is the spiritual center of the Eternal Flame and is known for master glassmakers and sacred stained glass.
Religion Aurelis
A member of the Order of Aurelis who is devoted to the service of the temples and the Radiants.
Clothing and Symbols Arm Cuffs Ceremonial bands worn on the upper arms that mark major bonds in a person's life. Nearly every citizen of Solarath wears a matched pair by adulthood.
Family Mother's Bells A strand of small bells worn around the waist or resting over the abdomen during pregnancy. Their sound is believed to guide a child's Heart Flame to their mother before birth.
